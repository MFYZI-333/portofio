What this site collects

MAFIOUZI is an editorial website. Its contact and newsletter forms currently show a local confirmation only; they do not send or store submissions.

Analytics and advertising

If configured, Google Analytics 4 and Google AdSense are loaded only after you choose “Accept optional cookies.” They may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, understand traffic sources and, where enabled by Google, deliver or measure advertising. Google Analytics may process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, and interactions. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into site search or forms.

You can reject optional cookies or change your choice using “Privacy settings” in the footer. Rejecting prevents these optional Google services from being loaded on future visits. Google may process data under its own terms and policies; review Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

External services

Images and fonts may be requested from third-party providers. Following external links takes you to sites with their own privacy practices.
